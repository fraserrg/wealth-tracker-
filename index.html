What the Numbers Are Telling You
🚀
You Are Extraordinary for Your Age
At 28, £73,100 in pensions puts you in the top 5–8% of UK savers your age. The average 28-year-old has £8–12K in a pension. You are 6x ahead of the median. This matters enormously because of compounding — a 27-year runway means your current pot will roughly 4x on its own at 5% real returns before you add another penny.
🏠
Property Is a Hidden Asset
Owning at 28 with £55K equity puts you ahead of ~75% of your peers. By retirement at 55, assuming modest 2% real house price growth, your home could be worth £650–700K with the mortgage paid off. This gives you an asset you can downsize from to supplement retirement income if needed — a meaningful backstop the projection doesn't even count.
⚡
Your SIPP vs Work Pension Split Needs Attention
You're putting £650/mo into work pension and £100/mo into personal SIPP. Check whether your employer matches contributions above the minimum — if they do, it's almost always worth maximising matched contributions first. Every £1 of employer match is a 100% instant return. Also verify your work pension fund — if it's a default fund, it may be in a lifestyling strategy that de-risks too early for a 55-target retirement.
🌉
The ISA Bridge Needs to Be Much Bigger
Your ISA (£10K + £400/mo) is designed to cover you from age 55–68 (13 years) before State Pension. At your planned retirement spend of £4,000/mo, you need £624,000 in accessible, non-pension assets for that bridge period. By 55 at £400/mo + 5% real growth, your ISA reaches approximately £230,000 — a significant shortfall. You'll need to draw some pension too (from age 57) or increase ISA contributions significantly after the mortgage is paid off.
📈
Every £100/mo Extra Is Worth £100K+ at 55
Due to compounding, an extra £100/mo invested from today at 5% real grows to approximately £62,000 by age 55. Every pay rise, bonus, or reduction in expenses you can funnel into investing now has an outsized impact. Even going from £1,150 to £1,500/mo total contributions increases your base-case retirement pot by ~£220,000.
🎯
The Critical Decade: Age 40–50
Your pot's most powerful compounding phase is roughly age 40–55. At 40, even the base-case shows ~£274K invested. That sum, untouched for 15 years at 5% real, becomes £570K on its own. This means career decisions in your 30s (promotions, salary growth, staying invested through volatility) disproportionately determine your retirement date. Don't derail the compounding in this phase.
